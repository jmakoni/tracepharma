# Partner onboarding

Filament classes:

- `App\Filament\App\Pages\PartnerOnboardingKitPage`
- `App\Filament\App\Pages\InviteTradingPartner`

## When to use

Invite a trading partner and share the onboarding kit (endpoints, sample files, contacts, checklist) for EPCIS / DSCSA connectivity.

## Prerequisites

- Partner company details (name, GLN, contacts).
- Integrations or master-data permission to invite.

## Steps

1. Open **Invite partner**; send the invitation with required fields. Open the page and use Help for live UI.
2. Open **Partner onboarding** kit; download or share checklist and sample artifacts.
3. Create inbound/outbound connections when credentials arrive.
4. Validate first documents and ATP readiness before go-live.
