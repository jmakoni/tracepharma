# Compliance alert center

Filament classes:

- `App\Filament\App\Pages\ComplianceAlertCenter`

## When to use

Triage open compliance signals (ingest failures, ATP gaps, expiry risk, investigation SLAs) from a single alert desk.

## Prerequisites

- Tenant user with compliance or investigator access.
- Alerts are generated by background jobs and inbound integrations.

## Steps

1. Open **Alert center** under Compliance. Open the page and use Help for live UI.
2. Filter by severity, category, site, or age.
3. Open an alert to jump to the related page (exception, partner, report, or worklist).
4. Acknowledge or resolve when the underlying issue is closed.
